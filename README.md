StudyFlow is a research-backed, gamified social study app built for the hackathon theme `Engagement & Staying in the Learning Journey`. It helps students stay motivated and keep learning over time instead of dropping off.

Built local-first with FastAPI, Next.js, SQLite and Ollama. It uses no paid APIs and no hosted backend.
Designed by me and developed with [OpenAI Codex](https://openai.com/codex/) as my coding agent.

## Why StudyFlow

Most learners don't quit because the material is too hard. They quit because it's hard to keep showing up alone. StudyFlow tackles this with three things that research links to persistence: [social accountability](https://doi.org/10.3102/0162373713500523), [regular routines](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1921417117) and [visible personal progress](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-019-09498-w).

## How it works

- **Study with your squad.** Friends join timed live sessions, each working on their own subject. Missed it? A solo session still counts.
- **Check your recall.** Every session ends with a 15-question quiz on your own topic, generated by a local LLM.
- **Keep the streak alive.** Squads share a daily streak, a goal for each session and a weekly points bar. Streak freezes protect against the occasional off day.
- **Compete on improvement.** Rankings compare each learner with their own baseline, never raw scores across different subjects.
- **Focus Tracker (optional).** An opt-in camera check that runs entirely in your browser. No video ever leaves your device, and it never affects your points.

## Quick start

Requires uv, Node.js 22+, npm, Ollama and make. Keep Ollama running.

```sh
ollama pull qwen2.5:7b-instruct
make setup
make migrate
make seed
make dev
```

Prefer Docker? Follow the [Docker setup](docs/setup.md#docker-option) for `.env` configuration and demo seeding, then run `docker compose up --build`.

Open [localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000). Demo login: `ada@demo.studyflow.example.com` / `StudyFlow-demo-2026!`.
